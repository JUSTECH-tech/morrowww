Delivery & shipping

How long does delivery take?

Orders placed before 2pm ship the same day. Inside Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt, TundaraExpress delivers in 1–2 business days. Other Nigerian cities take 2–4 days, and remote areas up to 6 days. You'll see a precise estimate on every product page and again at checkout before you pay.

How much does delivery cost?

Standard delivery is free on orders over the free-shipping threshold shown in your basket. Below that, rates start at ₦1,200 and are calculated by weight and destination at checkout. Express and scheduled delivery cost a little extra and are always shown upfront.

Can I change my delivery address after ordering?

Yes — as long as the order hasn't left our warehouse. Open My orders, choose the order and select “Change address”. Once a parcel is with the courier, you can still redirect it from the tracking page in most cities.

Do you deliver to pickup stations?

We do. Choose “Pickup station” at checkout and collect from one of 300+ Tundara Points when it suits you. Pickup is free, parcels are held for 7 days, and you'll get a code by SMS and email when your item arrives.

What if nobody is home?

The courier will call before arriving and try twice. After two failed attempts the parcel returns to the nearest hub and stays there for 5 days. You can reschedule from the tracking page at no extra cost, or ask us to leave it with a neighbour or security desk.

Returns & refunds

What is the returns policy?

You have 7 days from delivery to return most items for free, in their original condition and packaging. Fashion, electronics, home and beauty are all covered. Some items — underwear, opened cosmetics, perishable groceries, digital downloads and custom-made pieces — can't be returned for hygiene or licensing reasons.

How do I start a return?

Go to My orders, pick the item and tap “Return or replace”. Choose a reason, print the free label we email you, and hand the parcel to any courier or drop it at a Tundara Point. No printer? We'll send a QR code instead.

When will I get my refund?

We inspect returns within 24 hours of arrival. Approved refunds reach your original payment method in 3–5 business days for cards and instantly for TundaraWallet. Bank transfers can take up to 7 business days depending on your bank. You'll get an email at every step.

Can I exchange instead of refunding?

Absolutely. Pick “Replace” when you open the return and choose a different size, colour or variant. If the new item costs more, you pay the difference; if it costs less, we refund the balance. Replacements ship as soon as we scan your return.

What if my item arrived damaged or wrong?

Sorry about that — we'll fix it fast. Report it within 48 hours from My orders and attach a photo. We arrange free pickup, send a replacement on priority, and cover any return shipping. No need to argue with anyone.

Payments & pricing

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard and Verve cards, bank transfer, USSD, TundaraWallet, and Pay on Delivery in most cities. Pay on Delivery is available for orders under ₦150,000 and a small verification fee may apply for first-time customers.

Is it safe to pay on Tundara?

Yes. Payments run on PCI-DSS Level 1 infrastructure with 3-D Secure and tokenised cards — we never see or store your full card number. Every order is also covered by Tundara Guarantee, which holds your money until the item is delivered as described.

How do coupons and flash sales work?

Add items to your basket, then enter the code in the coupon box on the basket or checkout page. One code per order, minimum-spend rules apply, and codes can't be combined with each other. Flash-sale prices last while stock lasts and the countdown on the homepage is the exact deadline.

Why did my total change at checkout?

Checkout adds delivery for your address and 7.5% VAT where applicable, both itemised before payment. If a coupon was removed, the code may have expired, hit its usage limit, or fallen below its minimum spend. Your basket subtotal itself never changes silently.

Can I pay in another currency?

Yes. Use the currency switcher in the header to browse in NGN, USD, GBP, EUR, GHS, KES or ZAR. You're always charged in the currency you select, and the order confirmation locks in that rate — no surprise conversion fees later.

Orders & tracking

Where is my order number?

It's in the confirmation email and SMS we send the moment you pay — it always starts with TD-. Signed-in customers can also find every order under My orders. Lost the email? Enter the email you ordered with on the tracking page.

How do I track my parcel?

Open Track order and paste your order number. You'll see a live timeline from “Order confirmed” to “Delivered”, the courier's name, and a precise ETA. No account or login is needed.

Can I cancel an order?

Yes, before it ships. Go to My orders and choose “Cancel order”. If it's already with the courier, refuse the delivery or start a free return once it arrives — either way you're fully refunded.

Can I order without an account?

You can browse and build a basket as a guest, but you'll need an account or a quick one-field sign-up to check out. It takes about 20 seconds, keeps your order history in one place, and lets you track and return items without digging through emails.

Account & security

How do I reset my password?

Choose “Forgot password” on the sign-in screen and we'll email a secure reset link within a minute. The link expires after 30 minutes. If it doesn't arrive, check spam or ask us to resend it from support.

How do I update my details or addresses?

Everything lives in your account. Use Profile for your name, phone and email, Addresses to add or set a default delivery address, and Preferences to choose your currency, language and notification style.

How is my data protected?

We use TLS 1.3 in transit, encrypt sensitive fields at rest, and limit staff access to what's needed to serve you. Card details never touch our servers — they're tokenised by our PCI-DSS Level 1 payment partner. You can export or delete your data at any time from Privacy settings.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Privacy and choose “Delete account”. We'll confirm by email, then erase personal data within 30 days, keeping only records we're legally required to hold (such as invoices). Open orders must be completed or cancelled first.

Sell on Tundara

How do I become a seller?

Apply in about 10 minutes with your business name, CAC number (or ID for individuals), bank account and a few product photos. Most applications are reviewed within 48 hours. There's no listing fee; we take a category-based commission only when you sell.

When do sellers get paid?

Payouts land every Monday for orders delivered and past their return window. You can track every naira in the Seller Centre, download statements, and request an early payout for a small fee once you've completed 20 orders.

About Tundara

What is Tundara?

Tundara is a Nigerian-born marketplace for everything you love — tech, fashion, home, beauty, groceries and more — with fast delivery, fair prices and buyer protection on every order. The name comes from “tunda” — Swahili for fruit — a nod to harvest, abundance and good things arriving fresh. That's how shopping should feel.

Where do you operate?

We deliver across all 36 states and the FCT, with same-day service in Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt. We also ship to Ghana, Kenya and South Africa from our regional hubs, with local currencies and returns.

Press & media

How do I request a press kit or interview?

Email press@tundara.com with your outlet, deadline and angle. We reply within one business day and can provide logos, founder bios, product imagery and data on African e-commerce trends.

Affiliate programme

How much can affiliates earn?

Up to 8% of every completed order, paid monthly with a 30-day cookie. Creators, publishers and communities are welcome. Apply with your channel links; approvals usually take 2 business days and there's no minimum audience.

Terms of service

What am I agreeing to when I order?

That you're 18 or older, the delivery details you give are accurate, and you'll pay the total shown at checkout. Items are sold by Tundara or independent sellers; Tundara Guarantee and our returns policy apply on top. The short version: shop fairly, and we'll look after you.

Can prices or availability change?

Yes — prices, promotions and stock can change until you place an order. Once your order is confirmed, the price you paid is locked. If an item turns out to be unavailable, we'll offer a replacement or a full refund within 24 hours.

Privacy

What data do you collect and why?

Only what's needed to sell and deliver: your contact and address details, order history, device information and — with consent — analytics and marketing preferences. We never sell personal data. You can opt out of marketing in one tap from any email or in account settings.

Cookies

How do you use cookies?

Essential cookies keep your basket, login and currency working. Optional cookies help us understand what's popular and show relevant offers. You can accept or reject optional cookies on your first visit, and change your choice any time from the cookie link in the footer.

Accessibility

Is Tundara accessible with a screen reader?

We aim for WCAG 2.2 AA: semantic landmarks, visible focus states, keyboard-operable menus, alt text on product imagery and reduced-motion support. If something blocks you, email access@tundara.com and our team will fix it — and help you complete your order in the meantime.
